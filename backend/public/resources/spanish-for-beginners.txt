# Spanish for Beginners - Basic Phrases

## Common Greetings

### Basic Expressions
- **Hola** - Hello
- **Adiós** - Goodbye  
- **Buenos días** - Good morning
- **Buenas tardes** - Good afternoon
- **Buenas noches** - Good evening/night
- **Por favor** - Please
- **Gracias** - Thank you
- **De nada** - You're welcome
- **Lo siento** - I'm sorry
- **Perdón** - Excuse me

### Introductions
- **Me llamo...** - My name is...
- **¿Cómo te llamas?** - What's your name?
- **Mucho gusto** - Nice to meet you
- **¿De dónde eres?** - Where are you from?
- **Soy de...** - I am from...

## Basic Questions
- **¿Cómo estás?** - How are you?
- **¿Qué tal?** - How's it going?
- **¿Hablas inglés?** - Do you speak English?
- **¿Entiendes?** - Do you understand?
- **¿Puedes ayudarme?** - Can you help me?

## Essential Verbs
- **Ser** - To be (permanent)
- **Estar** - To be (temporary)
- **Tener** - To have
- **Hacer** - To do/make
- **Ir** - To go
- **Comer** - To eat
- **Beber** - To drink
- **Hablar** - To speak
- **Entender** - To understand
- **Aprender** - To learn

## Numbers 1-10
- **Uno** - One
- **Dos** - Two
- **Tres** - Three
- **Cuatro** - Four
- **Cinco** - Five
- **Seis** - Six
- **Siete** - Seven
- **Ocho** - Eight
- **Nueve** - Nine
- **Diez** - Ten

## Common Nouns
- **Casa** - House
- **Agua** - Water
- **Comida** - Food
- **Libro** - Book
- **Amigo** - Friend
- **Familia** - Family
- **Tiempo** - Time/Weather
- **Dinero** - Money
- **Trabajo** - Work
- **Escuela** - School

## Basic Adjectives
- **Grande** - Big
- **Pequeño** - Small
- **Bueno** - Good
- **Malo** - Bad
- **Fácil** - Easy
- **Difícil** - Difficult
- **Nuevo** - New
- **Viejo** - Old
- **Feliz** - Happy
- **Triste** - Sad

## Practice Phrases
- **¿Dónde está el baño?** - Where is the bathroom?
- **¿Cuánto cuesta?** - How much does it cost?
- **No hablo español muy bien.** - I don't speak Spanish very well.
- **¿Puedes repetir, por favor?** - Can you repeat, please?
- **Necesito ayuda.** - I need help.

## Tips for Learning
1. Practice pronunciation daily
2. Learn vocabulary in context
3. Listen to Spanish music and podcasts
4. Watch Spanish movies with subtitles
5. Practice with native speakers
6. Use flashcards for memorization
7. Focus on high-frequency words first
8. Be patient and consistent
