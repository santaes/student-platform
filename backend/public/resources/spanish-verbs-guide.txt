# Spanish Verbs - Essential Conjugations

## Present Tense - Regular Verbs

### -AR Verbs (Example: Hablar - To speak)
- **Yo hablo** - I speak
- **Tú hablas** - You speak (informal)
- **Él/Ella/Usted habla** - He/She/You speak (formal)
- **Nosotros hablamos** - We speak
- **Vosotros habláis** - You all speak (Spain)
- **Ellos/Ellas/Ustedes hablan** - They/You all speak

### -ER Verbs (Example: Comer - To eat)
- **Yo como** - I eat
- **Tú comes** - You eat (informal)
- **Él/Ella/Usted come** - He/She/You eat (formal)
- **Nosotros comemos** - We eat
- **Vosotros coméis** - You all eat (Spain)
- **Ellos/Ellas/Ustedes comen** - They/You all eat

### -IR Verbs (Example: Vivir - To live)
- **Yo vivo** - I live
- **Tú vives** - You live (informal)
- **Él/Ella/Usted vive** - He/She/You live (formal)
- **Nosotros vivimos** - We live
- **Vosotros vivís** - You all live (Spain)
- **Ellos/Ellas/Ustedes viven** - They/You all live

## Important Irregular Verbs

### Ser (To be - permanent characteristics)
- **Yo soy** - I am
- **Tú eres** - You are (informal)
- **Él/Ella/Usted es** - He/She/You are (formal)
- **Nosotros somos** - We are
- **Vosotros sois** - You all are (Spain)
- **Ellos/Ellas/Ustedes son** - They/You all are

### Estar (To be - temporary states/locations)
- **Yo estoy** - I am
- **Tú estás** - You are (informal)
- **Él/Ella/Usted está** - He/She/You are (formal)
- **Nosotros estamos** - We are
- **Vosotros estáis** - You all are (Spain)
- **Ellos/Ellas/Ustedes están** - They/You all are

### Tener (To have)
- **Yo tengo** - I have
- **Tú tienes** - You have (informal)
- **Él/Ella/Usted tiene** - He/She/You have (formal)
- **Nosotros tenemos** - We have
- **Vosotros tenéis** - You all have (Spain)
- **Ellos/Ellas/Ustedes tienen** - They/You all have

### Ir (To go)
- **Yo voy** - I go
- **Tú vas** - You go (informal)
- **Él/Ella/Usted va** - He/She/You go (formal)
- **Nosotros vamos** - We go
- **Vosotros vais** - You all go (Spain)
- **Ellos/Ellas/Ustedes van** - They/You all go

### Hacer (To do/make)
- **Yo hago** - I do/make
- **Tú haces** - You do/make (informal)
- **Él/Ella/Usted hace** - He/She/You do/make (formal)
- **Nosotros hacemos** - We do/make
- **Vosotros hacéis** - You all do/make (Spain)
- **Ellos/Ellas/Ustedes hacen** - They/You all do/make

## Common Verb Phrases

### Daily Activities
- **Me levanto a las 7:00** - I get up at 7:00
- **Desayuno temprano** - I have breakfast early
- **Trabajo en una oficina** - I work in an office
- **Estudio español** - I study Spanish
- **Ceno con mi familia** - I have dinner with my family
- **Duermo 8 horas** - I sleep 8 hours

### Questions with Verbs
- **¿Qué haces?** - What are you doing?
- **¿Dónde vives?** - Where do you live?
- **¿Tienes hermanos?** - Do you have siblings?
- **¿A qué hora vas a trabajar?** - What time do you go to work?
- **¿Hablas otros idiomas?** - Do you speak other languages?

## Past Tense Introduction (Preterite)

### Regular -AR Verbs
- **Yo hablé** - I spoke
- **Tú hablaste** - You spoke
- **Él/Ella/Usted habló** - He/She/You spoke
- **Nosotros hablamos** - We spoke
- **Ellos/Ellas/Ustedes hablaron** - They/You all spoke

### Irregular Past Tense
- **Yo fui** - I went (from ir)
- **Yo tuve** - I had (from tener)
- **Yo estuve** - I was (from estar)
- **Yo hice** - I did/made (from hacer)

## Future with "Ir a + infinitive"
- **Voy a estudiar** - I am going to study
- **Vas a trabajar** - You are going to work
- **Va a comer** - He/She is going to eat
- **Vamos a aprender** - We are going to learn
- **Van a practicar** - They are going to practice
